Design a high-fidelity mobile emergency safety app prototype for civilians in Inner Sogn, Norway.

The app is part of a fictional student innovation concept for a possible large passenger-ship collision near a harbor or kai.

This is NOT a professional command dashboard. It is designed for ordinary people: residents, tourists, workers, students, families and visitors who may be frightened, injured, confused, unfamiliar with the area, or unable to speak Norwegian.

The UX must be extremely simple under stress: very large typography, very large touch targets, minimal reading, strong hierarchy, clear icons, accessible contrast, one obvious primary action per screen.

Visual direction: modern Scandinavian civic design, calm, trustworthy, robust, clean, understated, premium public-service UX. Warm off-white and light neutral surfaces, charcoal text, muted fjord gray-green, deep safety green, restrained amber for warnings, emergency red only for critical danger. No purple, no lilac, no neon blue, no blue-purple gradients, no cyberpunk, no sci-fi, no generic AI aesthetic, no decorative glassmorphism.

Use realistic mobile UI proportions, polished product design, subtle depth, generous spacing, clear status bars, modern map styling, large emergency controls.

The app should feel believable enough to be presented to Norwegian municipalities and emergency stakeholders, while clearly remaining a fictional concept prototype.

Do not show advertisements, social media functions, chat clutter, unnecessary settings, or complex dashboards.

Use consistent branding:
SOGN SAFE
Civilian Emergency App

Keep all screens visually consistent as one coherent product family.
