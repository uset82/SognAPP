Small-size: master vs optimised variant

Master @ 32px  ·  4 bands
Variant @ 32px  ·  5 bands
Master @ 64px  ·  5 bands
Variant @ 64px  ·  4 bands
Why there are two, and the honest trade. At 32px the master's three wave layers merge into visual mud: the gaps between them are ~14 units at master scale, which is 0.44px when rendered at 32 — sub-pixel, so they vanish. The variant scales the symbol up 1.22×, which restores all five separate elements there.

But it is not a straight win: at 64px the variant is worse (4 bands vs the master's 5), because the 1.22× scale pushes the gaps back below one pixel at that size. Read the table below before choosing — neither variant dominates.
