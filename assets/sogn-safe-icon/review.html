Small-size: master vs optimised variant

Master @ 32px  ·  4 bands
Variant @ 32px  ·  5 bands
Master @ 64px  ·  5 bands
Variant @ 64px  ·  5 bands
Why there are two. At 32px the master's three wave layers merge into visual mud: the gaps between them are ~14 units at master scale, which is 0.44px when rendered at 32 — sub-pixel, so they vanish. The variant scales the symbol up 1.22× and widens the gaps to ~32 units, which restores all five separate elements. Both are shipped; pick whichever looks right to you.
