
Create a premium 3D brand identity and app-icon concept for:

SOGN SAFE

A civilian emergency-safety and evacuation app for Inner Sogn, Norway.

The logo must preserve the core visual idea of the reference image:
a minimal geometric symbol resembling the front bow of a ship viewed head-on, with layered horizontal shapes underneath that can simultaneously suggest water, navigation waves, radar/signal layers, direction and safe passage.

The mark should feel simple and instantly recognizable like a modern technology app icon, but it must NOT imitate or copy Cursor, Expo, Grok, X, or any existing brand.

The identity should communicate:

- maritime safety
- Norwegian fjords
- emergency preparedness
- navigation
- guidance
- protection
- technology
- calm authority
- reliability

STYLE

Create a refined 3D version of the symbol.

The 3D effect should be subtle, premium and architectural, not cartoonish.

Use:

- matte graphite black
- deep charcoal
- soft ceramic white
- subtle brushed-metal edge highlights
- extremely restrained dark navy undertone
- realistic soft studio lighting
- precise bevels
- shallow extrusion
- elegant depth
- ambient occlusion
- controlled reflections
- premium industrial-design finish

The upper shape should resemble a stylized ship bow approaching the viewer.

Use two angular side surfaces meeting at a central vertical seam.

Give the ship form slight 3D depth so the left and right faces catch different amounts of light.

Below the ship bow, create 2–3 clean layered geometric forms suggesting:

- fjord water
- navigation signal
- safe path
- radar waves

These lower forms should become progressively smaller toward the bottom.

The overall silhouette should remain compact, symmetrical and readable at very small app-icon sizes.

APP ICON

Place the 3D symbol centered inside a square iOS app icon.

Icon background:

very dark graphite / near-black
subtle matte surface
no gradient glow
no neon
no glassmorphism
no distracting texture

The icon should look excellent at:

1024×1024
180×180
60×60
small notification icon scale

Do not include text inside the actual app icon.

Create enough negative space around the symbol so it feels premium and recognizable.

TYPOGRAPHY

Below the icon, show the brand wordmark:

SOGN SAFE

Use a sophisticated modern geometric sans-serif wordmark.

Typography characteristics:

- clean Scandinavian design
- custom-feeling geometric letterforms
- strong but elegant
- medium-to-semi-bold weight
- slightly extended width
- excellent kerning
- generous but controlled tracking
- minimal
- contemporary
- not futuristic
- not military
- not generic startup typography

Make “SOGN” slightly heavier than “SAFE”, or alternatively use equal weight with increased tracking between the two words.

Avoid generic default fonts such as Arial, Roboto or plain Inter.

The lettering should feel closer to a premium Nordic transport, architecture or technology brand.

Optional visual direction:
custom geometric sans inspired by modern Swiss/Nordic typography, with subtly squared curves and distinctive G, A and F letterforms.

TAGLINE

Under the wordmark, optionally show a very small restrained tagline:

GUIDANCE WHEN SECONDS MATTER

or:

SAFER JOURNEYS AHEAD

Use uppercase micro typography with wide tracking.

The tagline must remain secondary.

BRAND PRESENTATION

Create a clean professional brand-board composition with:

1. Primary 3D symbol
2. iOS app icon
3. horizontal logo lockup
4. black-on-white version
5. white-on-black version
6. simplified flat monochrome version for small sizes
7. app icon shown on an iPhone home screen
8. small favicon / notification-scale version

Use a warm off-white editorial background for the presentation board.

Keep the layout minimal and Scandinavian.

No purple.
No lilac.
No neon cyan.
No AI gradients.
No cyberpunk.
No excessive glow.
No mock military symbols.
No shield unless presented as a very subtle optional variation.

The overall result should look like a real professional brand created for a Norwegian civic-tech / public-safety product.

Highly polished branding case study, premium industrial 3D rendering, realistic materials, precise typography, minimal Nordic design, app-store-ready identity.
